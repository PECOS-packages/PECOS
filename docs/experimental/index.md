# Experimental

Documentation for APIs in `exp/` and `pecos.experimental`. These are under active development and may change without notice.

- **[Composable Noise Models](composable-noise.md)**: Event-driven noise modeling with composable channels (`pecos-neo`).
- **[Experimental Decoders](decoders.md)**: Frontier and BP-Trellis, the only decoders that report a complementary gap, plus provenance-based hyperedge decomposition for matching decoders.
- **[Quantum Detector Models](quantum-detector-models.md)**: Detector-conditioned quantum instruments, persistent leakage/loss sectors, and exact coherent repetition-code generation.
